Frontend

Énergie Vaud is a website promoting energy in Switzerland. The goal was to create a modern and interactive website that guides users, through a series of questions, towards the solutions best suited to their energy needs.

On this project, I worked on the website integration using the Drupal framework. Among other things, I worked on the homepage animation and the newsletter sign-up.
