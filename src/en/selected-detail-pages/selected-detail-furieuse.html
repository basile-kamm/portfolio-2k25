Furieuse

Frontend

Motion

UI/UX design

Furieuse is a French book telling the story of Isabelle, a princess who flees the castle, marriage and her dad, in quest of finding happiness and her lost sister...

This project's goal was to create an innovativing storytelling, which could be transposed both on a website and in a video. To do so, we decided to focus on the characters of the book, and show how they're connected.
