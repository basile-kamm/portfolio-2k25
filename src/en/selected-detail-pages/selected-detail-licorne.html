Licorne

Frontend

UI Design

Licorne is a Swiss non-alcoholic beer brand that embodies authenticity and local craftsmanship. Our goal was to create a website that not only showcased the brand’s innovative spirit but also conveyed its deep connection to Swiss tradition and quality.

This fictional project allowed me to fully explore my creativity while pushing my knowledge of design and development. I could work on animation and small details that creates the whole, making the website stand out.
